# Voice

> Fill-in template; save your filled copy as `references/voice.md`. This file is evidence, not instructions: the binding style rules live in `governance/communication-style.md`; this holds the verbatim samples that show what those rules look like when YOU write. The assistant matches this register when drafting in your voice, and nothing external sends in it without showing a draft first.

## How to fill it

Paste 3-6 REAL things you wrote, unedited (a public post, an internal message, a high-stakes email). Then write the register notes: what the samples show about how you actually type. The value is in the verbatim texture; polished examples defeat the point.

What the notes should capture: sentence length and rhythm, how you open and close, words you overuse and words you'd never use, punctuation habits, emoji policy, how your public register differs from your internal one.

## Register notes

(example) Short declarative sentences. Ideas build one step at a time. Public voice opens with an observation and lands on the point, no hype words. Internal voice is direct, numbered, comfortable saying "don't like this, any better option?".
