# Lessons

Cross-project, empirical, domain-less lessons that don't warrant their own governance file. On-demand. Routing for where the next lesson goes lives in `memory-promotion.md`.

## Rules

- Hard cap: ~2000 words / ~12KB. Entries <=150 words each (~12 to 15 lessons). Enforced by `scripts/hygiene_check.py`.
- Budget pressure is the anti-bloat rule: when an add would exceed the cap, consolidate or retire an existing entry first. The file never just grows.

## Salience (what earns an entry)

- Empirical and hard-won.
- Applies to 2+ projects.
- Would change future behavior.
- Not re-derivable from the code.
- Not already covered by a governance rule.

## SKIP (does not belong here)

- One-off project trivia, re-discoverable facts, task progress, restating existing governance.
- Preference about how the assistant works -> `governance/` or `communication-style.md`.
- Identity fact about you or the business -> `context/`.

## Entry format

- `### <heading>`
- The lesson as imperative rules only. No story.
- `Why:` one line.
- `Provenance:` link to the decision, brainstorm, or session.
- `Date:` YYYY-MM-DD.
