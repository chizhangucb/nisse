# Tag Registry

The one controlled vocabulary for wiki tags (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`, Tags). Subjects only, never interaction properties. Check slugs AND aliases here before minting a new tag; add the row first, then use the tag. Rules: nouns, singular, max 2 words, underscores.

| Slug | Meaning | Aliases seen in the wild |
|---|---|---|
| (example) ci_pipeline | continuous-integration tooling and flakes | build system, CI/CD |
